# Prior work: bounded comparison

Primary sources were refreshed on 5 November 2026. The paper credits
Sigrist's powers-of-two description (OEIS A379719), Caveney's Fibonacci outline
(A377092), squares-plus-2 outline (A400196) and role-of-2 comment (A400197),
and trureturing/The Omega Institute's factorial interval-filling proof.
The factorial Lean source was inspected at commit
535a6648df930c3bfdca8a945bfcebdb0d0e1ec7, without an external build or axiom audit.
The interval-filling antecedent is not claimed as a first discovery here.

The general arbitrary-set theorem, exact allowed checkpoints and residue-tail
criterion were compared separately in a targeted follow-up with 16 queries.
No equivalent general statement was identified in successfully inspected
material. Many search hits were irrelevant. Full Caveney SeqFan correspondence
remains unavailable despite renewed retrieval attempts; unpublished overlap
is not excluded. This is a bounded source comparison, not novelty clearance.

at https://neilsloane.com/doc/. The latter PDF title page gives 10 September
2026; the publication list displays 2025 for that link. The paper follows
the title page. OEIS A377091 reports a separate square-walk proof announcement
by Sloane and Xausa on 29 October 2026, under review; that proof has not been checked as part of this work.

The prior base-seven paper is a separate work:

original distance set. This paper does not upgrade its claims or prove
